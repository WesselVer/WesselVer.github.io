    Personal project — Car

    Radiator upgrade

    To prevent overheating during spirited (or even regular) driving, I found a GTI radiator and swapped it into the car. I also flushed the cooling system.

    To fit the old fan, I had to weld a new bracket from 6 mm stainless steel.

    I also installed a new thermoswitch with a lower opening point, and it works amazingly: regular driving now sits at 80 °C, and racing at 90 °C.

    Brake upgrade

  • 2021 · Laser cutting · 3D printing

    Over-engineering a bierplank

    A custom beer board for my JC, designed in Onshape and still going strong five years later.

    2021 — Personal project

    Over-engineering a bierplank

    One of my most important projects: a custom bierplank for my JC. I designed it in Onshape with 11 holes, laser cut from two 6 mm sheets of wood that are cross-bonded together for strength. The centre piece is a laser-engraved 6 mm sheet of acrylic with a painted stencil behind it, which gives a coloured drop-shadow effect.

    Designed by me, made with a friend. It was a lot of work, but five years later it still performs amazingly.

    A later addition: 3D-printed inserts that hold the glasses better when walking through a full kroeg.
